## The Pattern

Across all 36 stories, the same barriers dissolve:

- **Age is irrelevant** — 15-year-olds and 60-year-olds equally empowered (#4, #5, #14)
- **Geography is irrelevant** — Nairobi, Lagos, Mississippi compete with Silicon Valley (#12, #13, #18)
- **Credentials are irrelevant** — No biology degree, no CS degree, no coding experience (#6, #9, #10)
- **Team size is irrelevant** — Solo founders match or beat funded teams (#1, #2, #3)
- **Domain expertise is irrelevant** — Outsiders solve problems insiders couldn't (#9, #16, #22)
- **Capital is irrelevant** — Two people outperform hedge funds with hundreds of analysts (#31)
- **Creative discipline is irrelevant** — Musicians make films, accountants build software (#6, #16)

**The constraint is no longer capability. It's imagination and initiative.**
