## The Program

Incubrain Mentorship is a **rolling monthly membership** for technical solopreneurs ready to execute. No batch model, no long-term contracts — join when you're ready, stay as long as you're growing.

### What You Get

- **Weekly group meetups** — Every Friday. In-person in Pune for local founders, hybrid for international members.
- **1:1 mentoring sessions** — Every other week. Focused, actionable, no fluff.
- **Async support** — DM access for quick questions between sessions.
- **Hosted Incubrain Founder** — Your personal support system, fully hosted and maintained.
- **Accounting guidance** — Practical help navigating the financial side of solopreneurship.
- **Access to compute, hardware & datasets** — Leverage the infrastructure you need without the overhead.

### Key Benefits

- **Save 40+ hours per month** — Through automation, outsourcing, and intelligent systems.
- **Massive clarity** — Better decisions, fewer dead-end paths, faster validation.
- **Full ecosystem access** — Guided training on Foundry, Founder, and Builder.
- **Elite network** — Maximum 20 founders. Every member is vetted and committed.

1. **Apply** — Fill out the application form. We review every submission personally.
2. **Onboard** — If accepted, you'll join the next available Friday meetup.

4. **Grow** — Stay as long as you're growing. Cancel anytime after the first month.
