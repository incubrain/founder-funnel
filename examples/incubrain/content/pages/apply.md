## Apply for Mentorship

We accept a maximum of **10 local (Pune/Maharashtra) + 10 international** founders at any time. Every application is reviewed personally.

### What We Look For

- **Technical background** — 3+ years of development, engineering, or AI/ML experience.
- **Execution bias** — You're ready to build, not plan forever.
- **Sector alignment** — Your idea intersects with AI and one of our 12 focus sectors (or has comparable exponential potential).
- **Commitment** — You're ready to invest time and money into your growth.

### Pricing

- **Local (Pune/Maharashtra):** $200 USD/month (~₹20,000)
- **International:** $2,000 USD/month

Single tier. No discounts. No free plans.
