## The Incubrain Method

We blend **Y Combinator-style execution**, **Peter Diamandis / Abundance360 exponential mindset**, and **practical open-source tools** into a system designed for technical solopreneurs.

The goal: help you leverage AI, compute, guidance, hardware, and datasets to outperform traditional 20+ person teams — solving cutting-edge problems in 12 key sectors.

---

## Three Pillars + Mentorship

Everything at Incubrain is built on three open-source pillars, tied together by the mentorship program.

### Pillar 1: Incubrain Foundry

**Prove your vision fast.**

Marketing site, blog, public business plan, decision log, and documentation — all in one open-source template. Deploy in minutes, start validating immediately.

- Capture email signups, presales, and bookings
- Event-driven analytics (swap providers without changing code)
- Webhook streaming to Slack, Discord, or your CRM

[Visit Foundry](https://foundry.incubrain.org){target="_blank"}

### Pillar 2: Incubrain Founder

**Your personal support system.**

Gap analysis, plan refinement, and founder tools — hosted for all mentees. This is the system that keeps you accountable and moving forward.

- Identify gaps in your business, skills, and strategy
- Refine your plan based on data, not assumptions
- Track progress and stay accountable

[Visit Founder](https://founder.incubrain.org){target="_blank"}

### Pillar 3: Incubrain Builder

**Ship real products.**

Ready-made foundation with authentication, databases, connectors, and more — all in one system. Skip months of infrastructure work and start building features.

- Auth, databases, and API connectors pre-built
- Designed for solopreneurs (not enterprise teams)
- Open source — extend it, change it, own it

[Visit Builder](https://builder.incubrain.org){target="_blank"}

## Mentorship Ties It Together

The tools are free. **The mentorship is where the magic happens.**

- **Weekly group meetups** — Every Friday. Accountability, peer learning, and high-signal networking.
- **1:1 mentoring** — Every other week. Focused on your specific challenges and next steps.
- **Async support** — DMs for quick questions. No waiting for the next session.
- **Hosted tools** — Incubrain Founder fully hosted and maintained for mentees.
