## Why This Matters

AI is not advancing along a single trajectory. It is advancing along **13 simultaneous, mutually reinforcing trajectories** — and they compound multiplicatively, not additively.

::callout{icon="i-lucide-trending-up" color="primary"}
**The compound rate is ~10-20x per year** in effective capability per dollar. Today's frontier capabilities will be essentially free by 2028, while new capabilities we cannot yet imagine will emerge at today's cost levels.
::

## How They Compound

These drivers don't add — they **multiply across five layers**:

### Layer 1: Raw Capability

| Component | Annual Rate | 2-Year Multiple |
|---|---|---|
| Hardware compute/$ | ~1.4x | ~2x |
| Algorithmic efficiency | ~3x | ~9x |
| **Combined** | **~4.2x** | **~17.6x** |

### Layer 2: Accessibility

| Component | Annual Rate | 2-Year Multiple |
|---|---|---|
| Inference cost reduction | ~10x | ~100x |
| Open-source proliferation | ~3x | ~9x |
| **Combined** | **Capabilities costing $1K today → ~$10 in 2028** | **~100x** |

### Layer 3: Application

| Component | Annual Rate | 2-Year Multiple |
|---|---|---|
| Agent capability | ~2-3x | ~4-9x |
| Tooling maturity | ~2x | ~4x |
| Enterprise adoption | Saturating → 95%+ | Near-universal |
| **Combined** | **Viable AI applications double every 6-8 months** | **~6-10x** |

### Layer 4: Funding

| Component | Annual Rate | 2-Year Multiple |
|---|---|---|
| Total AI spending | ~1.7x | ~3x |
| Enterprise AI revenue | ~1.8x | ~3.2x |
| **Combined** | **Resources flowing into AI double yearly** | **~3-4x** |

### Layer 5: Recursive Acceleration

::callout{icon="i-lucide-infinity" color="warning"}
AI-assisted R&D compresses development cycles by 2-5x. Frontier lab effective workforces are growing 5-10x via AI automation. This accelerates the improvement rate of **every other layer** — the meta-multiplier that makes the compound curve steeper over time.
::

## 2026-2028 Projections

### The Effective Capability Curve

| Timeframe | Capability Multiple | What This Means |
|---|---|---|
| End of 2026 | 1x (baseline) | Today's frontier capabilities |
| End of 2027 | ~10-20x | Today's frontier at 1/10th the cost; new capabilities at today's cost |
| End of 2028 | ~100-400x | Today's frontier essentially free; unimaginable capabilities at today's cost |

### By End of 2027

- AI agents complete multi-hour coding tasks with >90% accuracy
- GPT-4-level performance costs <$0.05/M tokens (effectively free)
- 70B+ models run on consumer hardware via quantization
- A single developer with agents matches a 10-20 person team
- AI-generated code exceeds 60-70% of all new code

### By End of 2028

- Autonomous agents handle the majority of standard app development
- Natural language specification replaces traditional programming for most apps
- 1 technical founder + AI = output of 50-100 person team
- Software development cost per unit of functionality drops 10-50x
- The distinction between "AI company" and "company" disappears

## Binding Constraints

::callout{icon="i-lucide-alert-triangle" color="error"}
**Energy is the single most binding constraint.** 74 GW demand by 2028 with a 49 GW shortfall. Data center moratoriums already in effect in Dublin, Amsterdam, and Singapore. Nuclear SMRs won't deliver meaningful capacity until 2029-2030+.
::

| Constraint | Severity | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Energy & power shortfall | High | $16B+ nuclear restarts, 10GW+ deals signed, but timing is tight |
| Training data exhaustion (~2028) | Medium-High | Synthetic data ($3.5B market), multimodal data, inference-time compute |
| Geopolitical fragmentation | Medium | Open-source creates resilience — released models can't be un-released |
| Agent reliability gaps | Medium | SWE-bench: <5% → 79% in 2 years — improving faster than predicted |
| Organizational absorption | Medium-Low | AI itself accelerates adaptation through better tools and agents |

## What This Means for Founders

### The Solopreneur Leverage Equation

| Year | 1 Developer Equals |
|---|---|
| 2020 | 1 developer |
| 2024 | ~3 developers |
| 2026 | ~10-20 developers |
| 2028 (projected) | ~50-100 developers |

::callout{icon="i-lucide-rocket" color="primary"}
**The next 24 months are a once-in-a-generation window.** The combination of transformative technology, plummeting costs, and not-yet-saturated markets creates conditions that will not repeat. Founders who act now ride the compound curve. Founders who wait compete against everyone who already did.
::

### Five Strategic Implications

1. **The build window is now** — what costs $10K today will cost $100 in 2028. Build while competitors hesitate.
2. **Team size is becoming irrelevant** — Midjourney: <100 employees, hundreds of millions in ARR. Master agent orchestration, not team management.
3. **The moat shifts from code to context** — domain expertise, proprietary data, distribution, speed, and trust are the new moats.
4. **Validate before building matters more than ever** — when building is cheap, finding the right thing to build is the only constraint.
5. **The window has a closing mechanism** — the same AI that empowers you today will empower everyone tomorrow. Early mastery compounds.
