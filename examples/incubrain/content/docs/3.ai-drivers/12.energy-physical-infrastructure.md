The AI revolution has an inconvenient dependency: electricity. Every model trained, every query answered, every agent that reasons burns watts. And the industry just hit a wall. Power availability has overtaken chip supply as the dominant constraint on AI scaling. The companies solving energy first will define the AI era.

::callout{icon="i-lucide-zap" color="primary"}
**Hyperscalers will spend $660-690 billion on infrastructure in 2026** -- a 36% increase over 2025, with roughly 75% targeting AI specifically. This is the largest capital expenditure program in human history. ([Introl Blog](https://introl.com/blog/hyperscaler-capex-600b-2026-ai-infrastructure-debt-january-2026))
::

::callout{icon="i-lucide-plug" color="warning"}
**40% of AI data centers will face operational constraints by 2027** due to power limitations. Projects over 100 MW face 5+ year grid connection delays. More than 36 data center projects representing $162B in investment were blocked or delayed as of mid-2025. ([EnkiAI](https://enkiai.com/ai-market-intelligence/ai-grid-constraints-2026-the-power-problem-halting-ai))
::

## AI's Energy Appetite

The scale of power consumption has entered a new order of magnitude. AI-optimized servers consumed 93 TWh in 2025 and will reach 432 TWh by 2030 -- a nearly **fivefold increase** ([IEA](https://www.iea.org/reports/energy-and-ai/energy-demand-from-ai)).

| Metric | 2024 | 2026 (Projected) | 2030 (Projected) |
|--------|------|-------------------|-------------------|
| **Global data center electricity (TWh)** | ~415 | 650-1,050 | ~945 |
| **% of global electricity** | ~1.5% | ~2.0-2.5% | ~3.0% |
| **AI share of data center power** | ~10% | ~25-30% | ~44% |

By 2035, data center electricity consumption will reach approximately **1,200 TWh** -- equivalent to Japan's entire national electricity consumption ([IEA](https://www.iea.org/reports/energy-and-ai/executive-summary)).

### Frontier Training Power Is Exponential

The power required to train cutting-edge models reveals the trajectory:

- **GPT-5 training** consumed an estimated **55-60 GWh** -- comparable to the monthly energy consumption of a city of 100,000 people ([Windows Central](https://www.windowscentral.com/artificial-intelligence/openai-chatgpt/gpt-5-is-powerful-but-hungry-1-5-million-us-households-energy))
- **GPT-5 daily inference** may consume up to **45 GWh per day**, enough to power 1.5 million US homes
- **Frontier training power doubles annually**, on trend to require **1-2 GW sustained power by 2028** and **4-16 GW by 2030** ([Epoch AI](https://epoch.ai/blog/power-demands-of-frontier-ai-training))

## The Nuclear Renaissance

Big Tech's pivot to nuclear is one of the decade's most dramatic energy developments. In aggregate, **tech companies signed over 10 GW of new US nuclear capacity** in the past year alone ([Trellis](https://trellis.net/article/amazon-google-meta-and-microsoft-go-nuclear/)).

| Company | Nuclear Deal | Capacity | Timeline |
|---------|-------------|----------|----------|
| **Microsoft** | Three Mile Island restart (Constellation) | 835 MW | 2028 |
| **Amazon** | Susquehanna PPA (Talen Energy) | 1.92 GW | Active |
| **Amazon** | X-energy SMR development | GW-scale | 2030 |
| **Meta** | Clinton plant PPA (Constellation) | 1.1 GW | June 2027 |
| **Google** | Kairos Power SMR fleet | 500 MW | 2030+ |

::callout{icon="i-lucide-atom" color="info"}
**Microsoft's Three Mile Island restart** is the first time a retired US nuclear reactor is being brought back to life for a single corporate client -- an 835 MW, 20-year PPA worth an estimated $16 billion, backed by a $1 billion federal DOE loan. ([NucNet](https://www.nucnet.org/news/constellation-secures-usd1-billion-federal-loann-for-three-mile-island-restart-11-3-2025))
::

**Geothermal is the sleeper play.** Fervo Energy is building an "Enhanced Geothermal Data Center Corridor" -- purpose-built geothermal power for AI facilities. Their Cape Station targets 500 MW by 2028, and Rhodium Group analysis suggests geothermal could serve **two-thirds of new data center demand** by 2030 at competitive prices ([Fervo Energy](https://fervoenergy.com/fervo-uipa-the-enhanced-geothermal-data-center-corridor-july-2025/)).

## Cooling: From Optional to Mandatory

AI chips generate dramatically more heat than traditional servers. Rack power density has pushed from 15 kW to 80-120 kW, with NVIDIA's Rubin Ultra targeting **600 kW per rack** by 2027.

| Cooling Technology | Heat Flux Capacity | Use Case |
|-------------------|-------------------|----------|
| **Air cooling** | ~10 W/cm2 | Traditional servers |
| **Direct-to-chip (cold plate)** | ~250-300 W/cm2 | Current GPU clusters |
| **Single-phase immersion** | ~250 W/cm2 | High-density training |
| **Two-phase immersion** | ~1,500 W/cm2 | Next-gen ultra-dense |

The liquid cooling market doubled in 2025 to ~$3 billion and is projected to reach **$7 billion by 2029** ([Dell'Oro Group](https://www.delloro.com/news/data-center-liquid-cooling-market-to-approach-7-billion-by-2029-as-ai-deployments-accelerate/)). Major acquisitions -- Eaton buying Boyd Thermal for $9.5B, Vertiv acquiring PurgeRite for $1B -- signal this is no longer bleeding edge.

## The Hidden Cost: Water

While energy dominates headlines, AI's water consumption is an emerging concern:

- US data centers consumed **66 billion liters** in 2023 -- a **3x increase** from 2014 ([EESI](https://www.eesi.org/articles/view/data-centers-and-water-consumption))
- Consumption could **double or quadruple by 2028** to 150-280 billion liters/year ([Brookings](https://www.brookings.edu/articles/ai-data-centers-and-water/))
- **Two-thirds of new data centers** are in regions already facing water stress ([Lincoln Institute](https://www.lincolninst.edu/publications/land-lines-magazine/articles/land-water-impacts-data-centers/))

::callout{icon="i-lucide-droplets" color="warning"}
**Training GPT-3 alone evaporated an estimated 700,000 liters of freshwater.** Large individual data centers consume up to 5 million gallons per day -- equivalent to a town of 10,000-50,000 people. ([MIT News](https://news.mit.edu/2025/explained-generative-ai-environmental-impact-0117))
::

## Key Takeaways

1. **Energy is the binding constraint, not chips.** 40% of AI data centers face power limitations by 2027. Projects over 100 MW face 5+ year connection delays. This single factor now determines AI's scaling trajectory.

2. **Nuclear is back.** 10+ GW signed by Big Tech in one year. Microsoft, Amazon, Meta, and Google are all pursuing nuclear PPAs and SMR investments -- a renaissance driven purely by AI demand.

3. **The spending is staggering.** $660-690B in hyperscaler CapEx for 2026 alone, with 75% targeting AI. The Stargate Project alone represents $500B committed. This is the largest infrastructure buildout in human history.

4. **Geothermal is the dark horse.** 24/7 carbon-free baseload power, rapidly declining costs, and the potential to serve two-thirds of new data center demand by 2030. Watch Fervo Energy.

5. **Environmental costs are real and growing.** Water consumption could quadruple by 2028. Two-thirds of new facilities sit in water-stressed regions. The tension between AI scaling speed and environmental responsibility is intensifying.

## Compounding Effect: How Energy Accelerates Every Other Driver

Energy is the **foundational layer** beneath the entire AI stack. Without power, no other driver functions. But the relationship goes deeper -- energy and AI create a self-reinforcing feedback loop:

| Cycle Step | Effect |
|-----------|--------|
| More energy available | Unlocks more compute capacity |
| More compute | Enables larger, more capable models |
| More capable models | Create new use cases, driving adoption |
| More adoption | Generates revenue justifying $660B+ annual CapEx |
| More investment | Funds more power generation and data centers |

::callout{icon="i-lucide-repeat" color="info"}
**AI is optimizing the energy that powers AI.** DeepMind reduces cooling energy by 20-40%. AI-guided drilling cut Fervo's geothermal well completion to 16 days. AI accelerates SMR design and renewable forecasting. The technology consuming energy is simultaneously making energy cheaper and more abundant.
::

Every energy breakthrough cascades outward:

- **Energy enables hardware scaling** -- more power means more GPU-hours for training and inference, directly translating to more capable models
- **Energy enables democratization** -- cheaper, abundant power reduces inference costs, making AI accessible to more founders and smaller teams
- **Energy enables research velocity** -- labs with guaranteed power supply can run continuous experiments without compute rationing
- **Energy enables geographic reach** -- solving power in new regions (Nordic hydro, Middle East solar, enhanced geothermal) distributes AI infrastructure globally

The risk is equally compounding. If energy constraints tighten, the cycle reverses: compute scarcity slows AI progress, reducing investment returns, lowering CapEx appetite, and leading to infrastructure stagnation. This is why energy is not just an operational concern -- it is an **existential constraint** on the AI industry's growth trajectory.

The roughly **$1 trillion** being invested in 2025-2026 alone is the industry's bet that this constraint can be solved. The companies, countries, and regions that solve it first will lead the AI era.
