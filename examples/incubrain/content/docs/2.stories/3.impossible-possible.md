## Beyond What Experts Thought Possible

These aren't incremental improvements. These are things that were considered impossible — problems no human could solve alone, fields no outsider could enter, barriers no individual could break. AI changed the definition of "impossible."

---

### A Dog Owner Designs a Cancer Vaccine

**Paul Conyngham** (Sydney), a tech entrepreneur with no medical background, used ChatGPT for research and AlphaFold to identify mutated protein targets. He worked with UNSW's RNA Institute to design a bespoke mRNA cancer vaccine for his dog Rosie. The tumor shrank approximately 75%.

This was the first-ever personalized cancer vaccine for a dog — designed by someone with zero medical training, guided entirely by AI.

:cited-source{url="https://fortune.com/2026/03/15/australian-tech-entrepreneur-ai-cancer-vaccine-dog-rosie-unsw-mrna/" label="Fortune"}

---

### Claude Solves What Donald Knuth Couldn't

**Filip Stappers** fed a Hamiltonian cycle problem to Claude that **Donald Knuth** — one of the greatest computer scientists alive — had been stuck on for weeks. Claude solved it in approximately one hour.

Knuth published "Claude's Cycles" with the opening: "Shock! Shock!" He wrote: "It seems I'll have to revise my opinions about generative AI."

When AI solves problems that stump legends, the ceiling for what a solo founder can achieve becomes essentially limitless.

:cited-source{url="https://cs.stanford.edu/~knuth/papers/claude-cycles.pdf" label="Stanford CS"}

### Two-Person AI Hedge Fund Doubles the Market

**Armina Rosenberg** and **Thomas Rice** launched Minotaur Capital — a hedge fund with zero analysts. AI processes 5,000 articles daily. They returned 42.4% net of fees, beating the MSCI by 9.3 percentage points.

Two people. No analysts. No trading floor. Better returns than firms with hundreds of employees and billions in infrastructure.

:cited-source{url="https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-02-13/hedge-fund-startup-replacing-analysts-with-ai-beats-the-market" label="Bloomberg"}

### AI Farming: 40% Less Spray, 18% Higher Yield

Individual farmers using AI pest monitoring are cutting chemical spraying by 40% and raising yields by 18%. The technology is affordable enough for smallholder farmers in developing nations.

This isn't agtech for Silicon Valley venture portfolios. This is AI directly improving livelihoods for people who need it most.

:cited-source{url="https://www.wiccagrow.com/ai-based-pest-detection-revolutionizing-crop-protection-in-2026/" label="WiccaGrow"}

## The Lesson

The common thread: **domain expertise is no longer a prerequisite for domain impact.** A tech entrepreneur designs cancer vaccines. A two-person team outperforms Wall Street. A smallholder farmer accesses the same precision agriculture as industrial operations.

AI doesn't just level the playing field — it removes the field entirely and lets you play wherever you want.
