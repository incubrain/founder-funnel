## The Window Is Open Now

Every technology wave has a window — a brief period where the early movers capture disproportionate value before the landscape solidifies. For AI-powered solopreneurship, that window is wide open right now. Here's the evidence.

---

### Teenagers Building a Billion-Dollar Company

**Cameron Fink** (18), **Ned Koh** (19), and **John Kessler** (15) founded Aaru — AI agents that replace consumer focus groups. They reached a $1B valuation. Combined founding team age: 52.

When three teenagers can build a billion-dollar company before they can vote, the message is clear: the traditional prerequisites for startup success — experience, network, capital — have been disrupted.

### Morgan Stanley: "A Shock Is Coming"

GPT-5.4 "Thinking" scored 83% on GDPVal, an expert-level reasoning benchmark. AI executives are privately telling Morgan Stanley investors to "brace for progress that will shock them."

When Wall Street's most influential research desk warns its clients that AI progress will be shocking, this is not hype — it's institutional acknowledgment that the pace of change is accelerating beyond what most people have internalized.

:cited-source{url="https://fortune.com/2026/03/13/elon-musk-morgan-stanley-ai-leap-2026/" label="Fortune"}

### Amodei's Prediction: 70–80% Odds on a One-Person Billion-Dollar Company

Anthropic CEO **Dario Amodei** puts **70–80% odds** on a one-person billion-dollar company emerging in 2026. Solo-founded startups already represent 36.3% of all new ventures, up from 22.2% in 2015.

This isn't a fringe prediction from a blogger. This is the CEO of one of the world's leading AI companies saying that the one-person unicorn is not only possible — it's probable, and it's imminent.

### AI Tutoring: 2 Hours Beats 8 Hours Traditional

Alpha School uses AI tutors where students complete academics in 2 hours per day. Average SAT score: 1535 (top 0.1% nationally). Sumner County public schools saw a 2x state improvement rate with AI tutoring.

The learning curve for AI skills is collapsing. What took years of training now takes months. The founders who start learning today will be unreachable by those who start next year.
