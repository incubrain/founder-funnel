## What is Foundry?

Foundry is an open-source [Nuxt Layer](https://nuxt.com/docs/getting-started/layers){target="_blank"} that gives technical founders everything they need to validate a product idea — landing pages, signal capture, event tracking, and webhook delivery — without building product features.
