## Overview

Each research domain identifies areas where Maharashtra-specific data is needed to translate global evidence into local policy. These gaps represent priority research activities for the pilot phase (2026-2029) and should inform the research agenda outlined in [Recommendation IV: Commission Detailed Policy Research](internal:policy/recommendations#4-commission-detailed-policy-research-and-drafting).

---

## 1. Ecology & Biodiversity

Research priorities for quantifying ALAN impacts on Maharashtra's ecosystems, agricultural systems, and coastal environments. See [Ecology & Biodiversity](internal:research/ecology) for the global evidence base.

1. **Baseline surveys**: Sky quality measurements across ecosystem types (Western Ghats, Deccan Plateau, coastal zones)
2. **Species monitoring**: Camera trap networks documenting nocturnal behavior changes under varying ALAN levels
3. **Agricultural assessments**: Pollinator abundance and crop yield correlations near artificially lit areas
4. **Marine ecology**: Turtle nesting success rates at Konkan coast sites (Velas, Anjarle, Dabhol)
5. **Economic valuation**: Ecosystem service losses attributable to ALAN (pollination, pest control, fisheries)

---

## 2. Human Health

Research priorities for establishing the health burden of ALAN exposure in Maharashtra populations. See [Human Health](internal:research/health) for the global evidence base.

1. **Baseline health data**: Urban vs. rural ALAN exposure levels and corresponding disease prevalence
2. **Occupational studies**: Shift worker health outcomes in Maharashtra's industrial and service sectors (Mumbai, Pune, manufacturing hubs)
3. **Pediatric assessments**: Child circadian development and urban light exposure in high-density residential areas
4. **Elderly populations**: Dementia risk and residential lighting patterns in aging Maharashtra communities
5. **Economic valuation**: Healthcare costs attributable to ALAN-related conditions in Maharashtra

---

## 3. Climate & Energy

Research priorities for quantifying Maharashtra's outdoor lighting energy waste and climate impact. See [Climate & Energy](internal:research/climate) for the global evidence base.

1. **Energy baseline**: Outdoor lighting electricity consumption by sector (municipal, commercial, industrial) and municipality
2. **Efficiency potential**: LED retrofit opportunity assessment across Maharashtra's public lighting infrastructure
3. **GHG quantification**: State-level greenhouse gas emissions from outdoor lighting using official grid emission factors
4. **Urban heat mapping**: Nighttime artificial light intensity and temperature correlation in major cities (Mumbai, Pune, Nagpur)
5. **Co-benefits analysis**: Integrated climate, energy, and biodiversity outcomes from lighting regulation scenarios

---

## 4. Tourism & Economy

Research priorities for assessing Maharashtra's astrotourism potential and economic opportunity. See [Tourism & Economy](internal:research/tourism) for the global evidence base.

1. **Demand assessment**: Tourist interest in dark sky experiences among domestic and international visitor segments
2. **Site inventory**: Sky quality mapping of potential astrotourism destinations across Maharashtra
3. **Feasibility studies**: Pilot site economic projections based on global case study benchmarks
4. **Best practices**: Adaptation of international astrotourism models (Northumberland, Utah, South Africa) to Maharashtra context
5. **Marketing research**: Target demographics, visitor personas, and promotional strategies for Maharashtra astrotourism

## Cross-Cutting Priorities

Several research needs span multiple domains:

| Priority | Domains | Rationale |
|----------|---------|-----------|
| Sky quality baseline mapping | Ecology, Tourism, Climate | Foundation for zone classification, site selection, and impact quantification |
| Economic valuation methodology | All | Cost-benefit framework needed for policy justification across all departments |
| Municipal lighting audit | Climate, Ecology | Energy consumption data serves both efficiency and biodiversity assessments |
| Vulnerable population mapping | Health, Ecology | Identifies communities at highest risk from ALAN exposure |
